Background

I am a retired Research Scientist with over 17 years of experience in academic research and university teaching in Physics and Engineering. I live in Leeds and have been successfully tutoring online for over 6 years.

Prior to my PhD, I was a voluntary researcher in Liquid Crystal studies at Lancaster University and in Astrophysics at Liverpool John Moores University. I also worked as a Physics lecturer at two universities in Tamil Nadu, India.

In the UK, my vast research and teaching experience across multi-disciplinary projects collaborated with leading industries, providing real-time insight into how science and engineering are applied in practice. I have taught masters modules, supervised research projects, and mentored doctoral students.

Having gained 17+ years of academic research experience, I also offer review services for academic articles and scientific manuscripts.

Tutoring Approach & Method

Inquiry-Based Learning for Confident, Independent Students

My goal is to enable tutees to turn into Confident & Independent learners and attain their highest academic potential in Physics, Chemistry, and Maths.

The student's prior knowledge is assessed, gaps are identified, and support is tailored to their personal learning pace. Using an inquiry-based learning method, students are taught how to solve problems while strictly adhering to exam board specifications.

Custom Past Papers & Student-Tailored Material

Tailored Exam Preparation: Standard past papers can sometimes be overwhelming or miss a student's specific areas for growth.

Dr Vasuki carefully studies older past examination papers and extracts key syllabus patterns to create custom-crafted practice papers. These materials are specially adjusted to match each student's current proficiency level, allowing them to systematically build problem-solving skills and master challenging topics before facing final exams.
